How it behaves
  • Groups fill a 7-day model. Mon–Thu writes the same hours to four days — the same per-day model the solver uses.
  • The sheet is the escape hatch. Edit any single day; a changed day detaches from its group and the group row shows “· Wed differs”.
  • Per-day “Closed” toggle handles closed days (e.g. Mondays) without breaking the group summary.
  • Post-midnight closes are first-class — native time picker, solver unrolls 1am/2:30am correctly.
  • Shift types stay out of onboarding — this captures open/close only; dividing a day into Morning/Evening/Late is a Scheduler job.
