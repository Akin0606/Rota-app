crewplan. staff — before / after

Side-by-side mockups for the visual changes in STAFF_DESIGN_REVIEW.md. Each pair renders the current behaviour (taken from the real source) beside the proposed version, using CrewPlan's actual .cp-staff tokens, Space Grotesk + IBM Plex Sans, dark-mode-first.

Note: contrast numbers in the review are token-derived; verify on a real build. "Before" is faithful to what the cited source renders today, not a strawman.

P0 · 1Muted / faint text contrast

Today --c-ink-faint (#4a4a47 ≈ ~2:1) carries real information at 10–11px — shift times, the "can't measure" caveat, pay sub-label, foot notes. The fix raises muted to meet 4.5:1 and reserves faint for a still-legible tone, so information stops disappearing.

P0 · 2Entry screen on the staff language

The first screen every staffer sees is the one surface not wrapped in .cp-staff: it uses heavier weights (extrabold/bold/semibold) than the two-weight rule allows and a drop shadow the language forbids. After: flat 0.5px-hairline card, weights 500/400 only, on-brand — matching the rest of the app.

P0 · 3Availability states — not colour alone

Today the three answered states (available / if needed / can't work) differ only by hue — a red/green-colourblind staffer can't tell them apart. After: a small glyph + micro-word inside each selected cell carries the state without relying on colour. (unset already differs structurally as dashed hollow.)

P1 · 4Touch targets ≥ 44px

Primary controls sit below the 44px minimum: the availability week pills (~32px, py-2 px-3) and the mode toggle (28px). After: pills grow to ~44px; the toggle keeps its 28px visual but gains an expanded hit area (dashed outline = tappable box).

P1 · 5Note-remove: literal ✕ → SVG icon in a 44px hit area
