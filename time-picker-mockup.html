Design & motion notes
  • Wheel over dial — pub times cluster on the hour/half-hour; a wheel is faster on a phone than a clock face.
  • Real physics — built on native scroll-snap momentum, so flicks feel right and cost nothing on the main thread.
  • Live readout + accent band — the centred selection is always framed; the big time updates as you spin.
  • Pub presets — one tap for 11am / 11pm / 1am; the wheel is there for anything else.
  • Post-midnight native — am/pm wheel reaches 1–2am cleanly; feeds the solver a real time.
  • Reduced-motion — snaps instantly, drops the scale/opacity flourishes.
