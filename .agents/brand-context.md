# Brand Context

## Brand
- **Name**: Rotally (formerly Crewplan)
- **Category**: Rota / staff scheduling software for small independent UK hospitality
- **Description**: Staff send their availability from one link; a solver writes the rota; the manager approves it; it emails out.
- **Stage**: Pre-launch — invite-gated pilot, onboarded by hand
- **Website**: rotally.co.uk (staging.rotally.co.uk)

## Audience
- **Primary Audience**: Owner-operators and general managers of single-site independent UK pubs, bars and restaurants. 6–25 staff, a high proportion part-time and under 18. The rota is written by one person, unpaid, at home, on a Sunday night.
- **Key Problem**: Rota-writing is invisible admin. Availability arrives in eleven unread messages; the diary is paper or a spreadsheet only one person understands; rest gaps and under-18 hours are checked in someone's head, if at all; a clash is discovered after the rota has gone out.
- **Their Language**: "rota" (never "schedule"), "on the book", "who's on", "cover", "shift", "swap", "the lad's under 18", "I do it Sunday night". Plain, fast, unsentimental trade language.

## Positioning
- **Differentiation**: Radical simplicity as the product, not a feature. Staff need no app, no account and no password — a link and four digits. The rota is genuinely *solved* (CP-SAT constraint solver), not templated. UK working-time and under-18 law is enforced at every write path, not offered as a report. And the roadmap states what is missing as plainly as what is built.
- **Competitors**: Deputy, Rotaready, 7shifts, Planday, RotaCloud, S4Labour — but the real incumbent is a WhatsApp group and a paper diary.
- **Market Position**: Niche and accessible. Deliberately not competing with enterprise workforce-management suites.

## Brand Personality
- **Personality Words**: Honest, plain-spoken, calm, competent, unfussy
- **Tone**: Casual but never chatty. Serious about the work, light about itself. British, understated, allergic to hype.
- **Voice Admires**: Monzo (plain-English honesty), Basecamp (opinionated restraint), Dishoom (British hospitality warmth without kitsch)

## Values & Mission
- **Core Values**: Tell the truth including what isn't built · Simplicity is the product · Respect the operator's unpaid time · Keep people legal by default · Earn trust before charging
- **Mission**: Give the person who writes the rota their Sunday night back.

## Goals
- **Primary Goal**: Convert hand-onboarded pilot venues into paying, retained customers
- **Key Metrics**: Pilot venues live · weeks published per venue · staff availability response rate · manager time-to-first-published-rota
