Before/after for STAFF_NAV_REVIEW.md, rendered at phone width (~375px). Before = today's hub-and-spoke with a BackButton on each spoke and no bottom nav. After = a persistent bottom tab bar (staff analogue of the manager app) + the hub reframed as a glance home.

Real .cp-staff tokens, Space Grotesk + IBM Plex Sans, dark-first, flat (no shadows on surfaces — the phone "shadow" is only the device bezel). Static design renders.

SPECThe proposed staff bottom tab bar

Five destinations (the ≤5 ceiling), staff analogue of components/manager/nav.tsx:57–80: fixed bottom-0, hairline top, flat, colour-only active state, env(safe-area-inset-bottom) padding, centred at the staff column width. Hours is demoted to Home + Shifts.
